Online OData Viewer

Free online OData visualizer. Enter the URL of an OData service, paste its $metadata document or upload the EDMX/CSDL file, and explore the service as an expandable tree, an entity-relationship diagram or syntax-highlighted source — entity sets, entity types, keys, properties, navigation properties with multiplicities, complex and enum types, functions and actions. OData V2, V3, V4 and V4.01 (XML and JSON) are supported.

The data explorer queries entity sets of the live service with $filter, $select, $orderby, $top, $skip and $count, follows next links and shows the exact request URL — also for services behind HTTP Basic authentication; credentials are forwarded per request and never stored. Sample services such as Northwind and TripPin are built in.

Sister tools by the same author: Online XSD Viewer and XML Online Viewer.

Guides: OData query options, SAP services, Power BI OData.Feed errors, all guides and resources.
